How it works

You set the rules — minimum profit, minimum return, how fast an item needs to sell. Bargainry does the looking.

  1. Collects the offers

    Retailer product feeds and clearance pages are checked several times a day for reduced, in-stock items.

  2. Finds the Amazon listing

    Each item is matched to its Amazon.co.uk page by barcode, with a title check when a barcode isn't available.

  3. Does the sums

    Amazon referral and fulfilment fees, delivery and prep costs, and sales-rank data go into one profit figure per unit.

  4. Tells you what's worth it

    Items that pass your rules arrive as alerts on Telegram, WhatsApp or email, with a link straight to the retailer's product page.

Retailers and how links work

Bargainry sends buyers to the retailer. Every alert and every row in the app links directly to the retailer's own product page, where the purchase is made at the retailer's price, on the retailer's site. Bargainry never resells, never caches checkout pages, and never shows a retailer's price without naming the retailer.

For retailers and affiliate networks

Where a retailer offers a product datafeed through an affiliate network, Bargainry uses the feed rather than crawling the site, and links through the network's tracked links. Reduced stock gets in front of buyers who are ready to purchase in quantity.

What Bargainry doesn't do

  • No scraping of sites that ask not to be, and no bypassing of access controls
  • No price-matching requests, voucher stacking or basket automation
  • No reselling, no storing of personal data from retailers' sites

Who it's for

Sellers starting out

You have a few hundred pounds, a spare evening or two a week, and no appetite for buying stock that sits in a cupboard. Bargainry shows you the handful of items each day that clear your profit bar, and nothing else.

Sellers scaling up

You already source from retailers' sales and want the comparison done before you get there. Connect your Amazon seller account to see restriction checks, exact fees and your own stock levels alongside each deal.
